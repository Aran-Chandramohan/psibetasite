Corporate Sponsorship

Sponsorship helps us host recruiting, workshops, service events, and networking opportunities for engineers at UT.

Bronze — $100

  • Your logo featured on our website
  • Featured in our newsletter
  • Mentioned in a social media post

Silver — $250

  • Your logo featured on our website
  • Featured in our newsletter
  • Mentioned in social media posts (2 or 3)
  • Access to our student resume database
  • Opportunity to host a sponsored event

Gold — $500

  • Your logo featured on our website
  • Featured in our newsletter
  • Mentioned in several social media posts (5 to 10)
  • Access to our student resume database
  • Opportunity to host a sponsored event
  • Opportunity to present at our general meeting

Platinum — $1000+

  • Your logo featured on our website
  • Featured in our newsletter
  • Mentioned in several social media posts (5 to 10)
